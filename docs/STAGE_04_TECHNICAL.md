# Stage 4: completed period technical structure

`technical_structure` is an additive report section. It groups verified daily observations into completed calendar weeks and months, excluding the week and month that contain the analysis date. Each timeframe shows its last completed session, five prior period support/resistance, a simple trend and breakout rule, and required sample size. The daily section adds a 20 session volume ratio and a 14 session mean range percentage. Future and invalid bars are excluded.

The 0–100 descriptive technical score has version `TECH-STRUCTURE-1` and explicit configurable category weights summing to 100: structure 20, trend 15, momentum 15, volume/flow proxy 20, volatility 10, levels 20. A missing weekly or monthly history yields PARTIAL. No score is a probability of profit or a trade authorization. Volume here is a price/volume proxy, never an observed buyer type flow.
