# Ten independent market lenses — 2026-09-27

The selection reflects criteria described by Iranian charting and brokerage
educational sites. It is **not a ranking of empirically best performers**.
All thresholds below are SMART's disclosed research rules. Each method is
calculated only from verified completed TSETMC daily OHLCV; invalid bars are
excluded and audited, and an invalid latest bar withholds all current votes.
There is no corporate-action adjustment guarantee, forecast calibration,
backtest claim for this set, or executable trade signal from vote counts.

| Method | Minimum valid bars | Research rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| SMA trend | 50 | Price > SMA20 > SMA50 is bullish, inverse bearish. |
| MACD | 35 | MACD(12,26) > signal(9) and > 0 bullish; inverse bearish. |
| RSI | 15 | RSI14 between 50 and 70 bullish; 30 to 50 bearish; extremes neutral. |
| Bollinger | 20 | Close between middle and upper/lower band (20, 2σ); outside neutral. |
| Ichimoku | 78 | Close above/below cloud and Tenkan >/< Kijun; cloud at t uses values at t−26. |
| ADX/DMI | 30 | ADX14 ≥ 25 with +DI > −DI bullish; reverse bearish. |
| MFI | 15 | Volume-weighted typical price; MFI14 50–80 bullish, 20–50 bearish; extremes neutral. |
| OBV | 21 | Positive/negative change in signed cumulative volume over 20 bars. |
| Breakout + volume | 21 | Close beyond previous 20-bar high/low AND current volume ≥ 1.2 × prior average. |
| ATR regime | 35 | ATR14 vs preceding 20-bar true-range mean: ≥1.5 is elevated risk; no directional vote. |
